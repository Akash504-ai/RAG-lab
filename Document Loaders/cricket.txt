🏏 The Heartbeat of Cricket
A poem celebrating the passion, emotions, dreams, and spirit of cricket.

The morning sun begins to rise,
A golden glow fills up the skies.
The field is ready, the pitch is green,
The greatest game has just begun its scene.

A little boy holds a wooden bat,
Dreaming of stadiums, imagine that!
A tennis ball, a dusty street,
A million dreams beneath his feet.

The bowler walks a little far,
The batter dreams of becoming a star.
The fielders wait with eyes so bright,
The crowd is ready for a fight.

The umpire raises his hand in the air,
A moment of silence fills everywhere.
The bowler runs with all his might,
The batter stands prepared to fight.

A ball comes racing, fast and straight,
The batter swings, decides his fate.
A beautiful shot flies over the ground,
And suddenly cheers are all around.

A boundary races across the grass,
The crowd erupts as moments pass.
A six goes flying into the sky,
Like a dream that refuses to die.

The scoreboard changes, the numbers grow,
The players move with a steady flow.
Each single run has its own story,
Each little victory brings its glory.

But cricket isn't always about the win,
Sometimes the hardest battles are within.
A dropped catch, a missed-out run,
Can turn the game before it's done.

A bowler watches the batter's eyes,
Planning a delivery in disguise.
A slower ball, a perfect line,
A wicket falls, the moment divine.

The stumps go flying, bails take flight,
The stadium bursts with pure delight.
The batter walks with a heavy heart,
Knowing another innings must start.

The captain stands with a thoughtful face,
Adjusting fielders, changing pace.
A game of patience, a game of mind,
Where courage and wisdom combine.

Sometimes the clouds cover the sun,
And rain interrupts the game of fun.
But fans remain, they never leave,
For cricket is something they believe.

From village grounds to stadium lights,
From local matches to international fights.
The passion travels far and wide,
With millions watching side by side.

The little streets remember the sound,
Of plastic stumps placed on the ground.
A broken window, a neighbour's shout,
Yet every evening, they still come out.

The younger brother wants to bat,
The older one says, "Don't do that!"
But soon they're playing, laughing again,
For cricket washes away every pain.

The radio speaks of distant lands,
While children hold their bats in hands.
They dream of wearing their country's pride,
With millions cheering by their side.

The national colours shine so bright,
The players walk into the light.
A flag waves proudly in the air,
A nation's hopes are everywhere.

The anthem plays, the crowd stands tall,
One shared dream connects them all.
Eleven players, one common aim,
To bring back victory and earn the fame.

A spinner turns the ball with grace,
Leaving the batter lost in space.
A googly comes, a leg-break spins,
And suddenly the bowler wins.

The wicketkeeper waits behind,
With lightning reflexes and a focused mind.
A sharp appeal echoes through the air,
The umpire's finger rises with care.

The final overs begin to unfold,
The pressure rises, the night turns cold.
Ten runs needed, six balls to go,
The crowd is moving to and fro.

The bowler breathes, the batter stares,
The whole stadium forgets its cares.
The first ball flies, a single is done,
Five more deliveries, the race isn't won.

The second ball travels high and far,
A glorious six beneath the stars.
The third one finds the boundary rope,
The crowd explodes with endless hope.

Two balls remaining, three runs to chase,
Sweat and tension cover every face.
The fourth ball comes, the batter swings,
A mighty shot, the stadium sings.

The fifth ball brings a single run,
Now everything depends on the one.
The final ball, the final chance,
The batter prepares for the ultimate dance.

The ball is bowled, the bat comes down,
It races past the fielders around.
The players run, the crowd goes wild,
A moment remembered since childhood.

The winning runs are finally scored,
The captain lifts the trophy adored.
The players hug, their eyes shine bright,
Their hard work wins the biggest fight.

Some celebrate, while others cry,
Some look up towards the sky.
For victory tastes sweeter after pain,
And every loss brings strength again.

The game may end, the lights may fade,
But memories never truly degrade.
The bat goes back, the stumps come down,
Yet cricket lives in every town.

Tomorrow brings another day,
Another match, another play.
New heroes rise, new dreams ignite,
The love of cricket burns so bright.

For cricket is more than runs and scores,
More than sixes, wickets, and applause.
It's friendship, courage, hope, and pride,
A beautiful journey shared worldwide.

And when the final chapter's written,
When every record has been bitten,
One truth will always remain the same:
The world will never forget this game. 🏏
